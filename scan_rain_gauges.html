Data retrieval and calculation

Hourly precipitation in inches (PRCP) was retrieved through the official AWDB API. Timestamps mark the end of each interval. Station metadata use UTC−4. Totals sum valid readings from the start of the event through each completed hour.

The archived SCAN display data are provided in scan_gauge_data.js. The USGS station records are listed in the separate USGS inventory.
