Language support

The original dashboard was developed in Spanish. Spanish is the default.
The language selector in the upper-right corner switches to English.
Open index.html?lang=en to start directly in English.

Changing languages preserves the selected event, frame, playback state,
playback speed, zoom and track visibility. The observations, units and
imagery are shared across languages. Official site and place names retain
their spelling. West longitude is abbreviated O in Spanish and W in English.

index.html contains the Spanish interface and information panels. i18n.js
localizes the controls and dynamic labels. notes.en.js contains the English
information panels, and catalogue.en.js translates the linked station
catalogues. The language selector applies to the interactive dashboard.
