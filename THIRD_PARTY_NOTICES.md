## Observations and geographical data

- GOES-16 ABI imagery: NOAA. https://registry.opendata.aws/noaa-goes/
- Stage IV precipitation estimates: NOAA NWS/NCEP, archived by NSF NCAR.
  https://water.noaa.gov/about/precipitation-data-access
  https://gdex.ucar.edu/datasets/d507005/
- Rain-gauge observations: USGS. https://waterdata.usgs.gov/
- Tropical-cyclone tracks and intensity: NOAA National Hurricane Center,
  HURDAT2. https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/data/#hurdat
- Puerto Rico boundary and municipalities: U.S. Census Bureau.
  https://www.census.gov/geographies/mapping-files/2024/geo/carto-boundary-file.html

These materials retain their source-specific conditions. No exclusive
copyright or commercial-use restriction is asserted here over the original
government observations, geographical facts, or public-domain records.
Technical source details and limitations are provided in
METODOLOGIA_Y_FUENTES.txt and in the bilingual information windows.

The Stage IV fields and the separately displayed USGS observations must not
automatically be treated as independent measurements. The downloaded Stage IV
files do not identify every contributing station or satellite for each hour.
