Historical Tropical Cyclone Dashboard for Puerto Rico
Xavier A. García-López

The project brings together GOES-16 imagery, NHC tracks and intensity,
Stage IV precipitation estimates, and USGS rain-gauge readings for
Irma and Maria (2017), Fiona (2022), and Ernesto (2024).

Open index.html at the repository root. Each storm has 168 frames selected
at approximately 30-minute intervals. GOES acquisition timestamps are
preserved in UTC and displayed in Puerto Rico time (UTC−4). NHC positions
are interpolated between official records. Intensity is drawn from the last
official record at the time shown. The hourly rainfall surface and the
rain-gauge series update at their respective observation intervals.

The olive outline is from U.S. Census Bureau cartography and remains visible
when the track is hidden. The north arrow and kilometer scale are in the
satellite panel. See processing/README.md for the imagery recipe, data sources
and reproduction instructions. The in-app notes describe the interpretation
and limitations of the rainfall estimates and station records.
